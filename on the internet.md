### IP Leaks, Extortion, and Cyber-Intimidation

My advice on this one is short as well: if you don't get scammed in the first place, you are far less likely to be extorted.

Scammers will try to make you feel like the public data they scraped is extremely sensitive such as your IP address.

Literally: **Do not care about anything they tell you.**
